Complaints Handling Policy

How we receive, investigate, and resolve customer complaints, and the timeframes you can expect.

Policy pending confirmation. This page is not yet in effect. It is awaiting formal review and sign-off before it becomes Solve Energy's official complaints handling policy. If you have a complaint in the meantime, please get in touch with us directly and we'll assist you personally.

1. Our commitment

We treat every complaint seriously, whether it's about a quote, an installation, a product fault, or how we've communicated with you. Making a complaint is free, and it won't affect the service or support you receive from us.

2. How to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint by submitting a ticket, through our contact page, or by phone. Please include your name, order or account reference, and as much detail as possible about the issue.

3. Acknowledgement

We'll acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days and let you know who's handling it.

4. Investigation

We'll investigate the issue, which may include contacting the installer, checking system data, or requesting further information from you. We aim to keep you updated at each meaningful step rather than leaving you waiting.

5. Resolution timeframe

Most complaints are resolved within 15 business days. If a complaint is more complex and needs longer, we'll tell you why and give you a revised timeframe.

6. If you're not satisfied

If you're not happy with how your complaint has been handled, you can ask for it to be reviewed by a more senior member of our team. If it's still unresolved, you may be able to escalate it to your state's fair trading body or an applicable energy/solar industry ombudsman scheme.

7. Record keeping

We keep a record of all complaints and how they were resolved, which helps us identify and fix recurring issues.
