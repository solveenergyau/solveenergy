Common variables

Questions people
actually ask

If yours isn't here, the quote form below reaches a person, not a queue.

Do I need battery storage, or just panels?
Not necessarily. If you're mostly home and using power during the day, panels alone can offset most of your bill. Battery storage matters more if you're out during daylight hours and using power at night — we'll model your specific usage before recommending one.
What happens on a cloudy day, or at night?
Panels still generate on overcast days, just at reduced output. At night, or when you use more than the panels produce, you draw from the grid as normal — or from a battery if you have one installed.
How much does an install actually cost?
It depends on system size, roof complexity and your existing switchboard. We give a fixed, itemised quote after the site assessment — not a "starting from" number designed to get you on the phone.
What if I generate more than I use?
Excess power is exported to the grid, and most retailers credit you for it under a feed-in tariff. We'll walk you through which tariff applies to your address during the assessment.
Do you handle rebates and paperwork?
Yes — council approval, grid-connection applications and any relevant government rebate paperwork are handled as part of the install, not as an add-on.
