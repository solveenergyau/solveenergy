Panels still generate under cloud, at a reduced rate. Overnight you draw from the battery if you have one, and from the grid once it's empty. Nothing switches off — the grid connection stays as a backstop, which is why the modelled day still shows some electricity bought.

It depends on array size, roof access, switchboard condition and whether storage is included, which is why we won't publish a “from” figure. After the site assessment you get one fixed number, itemised, and it doesn't move afterwards.

Surplus goes to the grid and you're credited at your retailer's feed-in rate, which is well below what you pay to buy it back. That gap is the whole argument for using your own generation on site, and the reason we size to consumption rather than to roof space.
